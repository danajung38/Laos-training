The Project for Establishment of Human Resource Development of Digital Innovative MSMEs in Lao PDR

A 4-month intensive capacity building training program for Lao government officials, designed to strengthen entrepreneurship and market-oriented business planning capacity through the Kauffman PEV (Planning Entrepreneurial Venture) framework.

📅 4 Months Mar – Jul 2026 · In Korea
🌐 English Language of Instruction
🏛️ Kookmin Univ. Seoul, Republic of Korea
👥 4 – 8 People Lao Government Officials

Program Objectives

Short-term

01

Strengthen the capacity of Lao government officials in entrepreneurship and market-oriented business planning through structured, hands-on training.

02

Develop locally implementable business plans reflecting the industrial and market environment of Lao PDR through the application of the Kauffman PEV framework.

03

Enhance policy and project planning capacity of government officials at the Ministry of Industry and Commerce (MOIC/MSMEPA), enabling more effective economic policy design.

Mid- to Long-term

04

Cultivate specialized policy planning personnel in entrepreneurship and SME development, establishing a human resource pool aligned with future ODA projects by KOICA and other development partners.

05

Build a sustained Korea–Lao PDR cooperation network in entrepreneurship education and policy, contributing to long-term bilateral development cooperation.

Key Components & Program Structure

This program moves beyond conventional lecture-based instruction, adopting a workshop-oriented approach that integrates scenario-based learning and project-centered training. Every module incorporates worksheets and team projects to ensure practical application. The core deliverable is a complete business plan tailored to the Lao PDR market.

📖

PEV Lectures

Weekly lectures following the Kauffman PEV framework — from opportunity recognition to financial planning and final business plan presentation.

🛠️

Workshops & Scenario Learning

Hands-on workshops with real Lao market scenarios. Participants apply each PEV chapter to their own business context through guided worksheets.

🏢

Field Trips

Visits to Korea's leading startup ecosystems — Asan Nanum Foundation, D.Camp, TIPS Town, KaKao, Startup Alliance, and Xplore Investment — to connect theory with practice.

🎤

Guest Speakers

Industry practitioners, serial entrepreneurs, and professors share real-world insights on startup building, market analysis, and investment readiness.

📋

Team Project

Participants work in teams from Week 2 onward, progressively building a full business plan. Mid-term and final presentations evaluate progress at each stage.

🚀

Grand IR Pitching

Final event where participants pitch their completed business plans to investors and stakeholders, simulating real-world funding and policy environments.

Kauffman PEV Framework

PEV · Planning Entrepreneurial Venture

A Proven Global Framework for Entrepreneurship Education

The Kauffman PEV (Planning Entrepreneurial Venture) program is a comprehensive, step-by-step entrepreneurship curriculum developed by the Kauffman Foundation. Operated reliably for over 20 years within universities and business education institutions worldwide, it guides participants through the entire venture creation process — from opportunity recognition and business model design to financial planning and investor pitching.

Unlike passive lecture formats, PEV is built around scenario-based learning and progressive project work, ensuring participants can directly apply concepts to their own business contexts. This program adapts the PEV framework to the Lao PDR market environment, making the outcomes immediately applicable to local policy and entrepreneurship development.

About the Kauffman Foundation

The Ewing Marion Kauffman Foundation, headquartered in Kansas City, USA, is the world's largest foundation dedicated to entrepreneurship. Established in 1966, it invests in education and entrepreneurship to foster innovation, economic growth, and opportunity — particularly for underserved communities. The Foundation's research, programs, and policy work have shaped entrepreneurship education globally, including the PEV curriculum adopted in this training program.
